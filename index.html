What We Make

Our Diamond Shapes

All diamonds are 100% natural and manufactured directly at our Surat facility. Available in a wide range of sizes to suit every jewellery design.

Round

Round Brilliant

The most popular and timeless diamond shape. Features a circular outline with brilliant-style facets that maximise light reflection and sparkle. Ideal for solitaire rings, earrings, and pendants.

⌀ 0.93 mm – 5.00 mm

Choki

Baguette Cut

A rectangular step-cut diamond with clean, parallel facets. Known locally as "Choki", this shape is widely used as side stones in rings, bracelets, and channel-set jewellery for an elegant, sleek look.

Length: 1.5 mm – 4.0 mm

Stubby Choki

Short Baguette

A wider, shorter variation of the classic Choki/Baguette. Featuring the same clean step-cut facets in a more square-proportioned rectangle. Ideal for pavé rows, half-eternity bands, and compact channel settings in bracelets and rings.

Length: 1.5 mm – 3.0 mm

Pear

Teardrop Cut

A graceful combination of the round and marquise shapes, with one pointed end and one rounded end. The pear shape adds elegance to pendants, earrings, and unique engagement ring designs.

Available in multiple sizes

Marquise

Navette Cut

An elongated shape with pointed ends at both sides, resembling a boat or eye. The marquise shape makes fingers appear longer and slender, and is popular in rings, earrings, and floral jewellery designs.

Available in multiple sizes

Princess

Square Brilliant

A square or slightly rectangular diamond with brilliant-style facets that deliver exceptional sparkle. One of the most modern and sought-after shapes for engagement rings, studs, and contemporary jewellery.

Available in multiple sizes
