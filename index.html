Our House

A Foundation Built on Precision & Trust

Shiv Shakti Diamonds manufactures natural loose diamonds at the heart of Surat — the city that cuts the majority of the world's diamonds. Every stone we produce reflects a discipline of precise cutting, honest grading, and consistent quality.

We work directly with international wholesalers, jewellery manufacturers, and luxury brands — removing the middlemen so our partners receive factory-direct value, transparent pricing, and a supply they can rely on, parcel after parcel.
